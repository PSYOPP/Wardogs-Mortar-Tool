Math

Minus signs don't matter, squaring removes them. Bearing is measured clockwise from map North (Y+): bearing = atan2(ΔX, ΔY).

Quick reference
  • L81 Mortar: up to ~700 m. Load up to 3 shells with R. 30 ammo per shell, 90 for a full barrage. Unlock: first hour. Cost: 91 building supplies plus hammer swings.
  • Artillery Tank: 750 m minimum, ~2,700 m maximum effective range. Quick-time-event reload. Much larger blast. Under 750 m it cannot reach, that's the mortar's job.
  • Artillery Tank mode (toggle above): the mini map grows to fit the shot and the badge judges range against the tank's 750 to 2,700 m envelope instead of the mortar's. Distance and direction use the exact same maths for both guns: Pythagoras on the map coordinates, × 100 for metres. The only difference is the envelope the shot must fall inside.
  • Aim: middle-click the target to drop the white diamond, centre the diamond in your view, set the RNG slider. Between two notches? Split the difference.
  • Load 3 shells before bombarding. Press C to skip the killcam. Reload during the killcam to keep continuous fire. Nudge aim between shells to spread damage.
  • Terrain: shells clip towers, trees and ridges. Short on an obstacle? The maths is fine, move the gun and re-run the numbers.
  • Map: each small square is 10 m.
